# Mantra 100-verb plan

Selection criteria:
- Modern Hebrew frequency (core/high/medium/low bands).
- Communicative usefulness for an adult learner living in Israel.
- Coverage of Pa'al, Pi'el, Hif'il, Hitpa'el, Nif'al, and a small number of Huf'al/Pual forms.
- Coverage of common irregular patterns: initial alef, final he, initial yod/nun drop, gutturals, weak roots.
- All Pealim dict URLs will be verified before content is marked complete.
